# Rules Glossary

Terms used by specs, data files and code. One entry per term. `Source` cites
PDF page numbers of `inspired/manual_eng.pdf` (Esoteric Order of Gamers rules
summary v5.6) and `inspired/EH_reference.pdf` (official Russian reference
guide). The reference guide is authoritative; where the two differ, the entry
states the reference reading and notes the summary's wording. Russian names
marked *(unofficial)* are expansion terms absent from the core reference guide.

Skill symbols in the reference guide text: `[` Lore, `]` Influence, `|`
Observation, `}` Strength, `{` Will, `@` reckoning.

## Round structure

### Round / Раунд
One pass through the Action, Encounter and Mythos phases in that order; rounds repeat until the investigators win or lose.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; EH_reference.pdf p.20

### Action Phase / Фаза действий
First of the three phases of a round. Starting with the lead investigator and going clockwise, each investigator performs up to 2 different actions; each action at most once per round and fully resolved before the next.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; EH_reference.pdf p.3, p.20

### Encounter Phase / Фаза контактов
Second phase. Starting with the lead investigator and going clockwise, each investigator resolves one encounter on their space: a combat encounter against each monster there, otherwise a location or token encounter of their choice.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; EH_reference.pdf p.5, p.20

### Mythos Phase / Фаза Мифа
Third phase. The lead investigator draws the top mythos card and resolves its effects in printed order. At the end of the phase the lead investigator may pass the lead investigator token.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; EH_reference.pdf p.6, p.20

### Lead investigator / Ведущий сыщик
Holder of the lead investigator token. Acts first in the Action and Encounter phases, draws the mythos card, makes final group decisions, and is the active investigator during the Mythos phase.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3, p.4; EH_reference.pdf p.3

### Active investigator / Активный сыщик
The investigator currently performing actions or resolving an encounter. In the Mythos phase, the lead investigator.
Source: EH_reference.pdf p.12

## Actions

### Travel / Путешествие
Move to an adjacent space, then spend any number of travel tickets to move 1 more space per ticket: train tickets only along train paths, ship tickets only along ship paths. Paths leaving the board edge continue on the opposite edge.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; EH_reference.pdf p.10

### Rest / Отдых
Recover 1 health and 1 sanity. Not allowed on a space with a monster.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; EH_reference.pdf p.7

### Trade / Обмен
Exchange possessions with another investigator on the same space when both agree. Conditions, health, sanity and improvement tokens cannot be traded; a defeated investigator cannot trade.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; EH_reference.pdf p.6

### Prepare for Travel / Подготовка к путешествию
On a city space, gain 1 travel ticket (train or ship) of a type whose path connects to the space. Maximum 2 tickets; excess is discarded.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; EH_reference.pdf p.8

### Acquire Assets / Приобретение активов
On a city space without monsters, test Influence and gain reserve cards whose total value is at most the test result; if none is gained, 1 reserve card may be discarded. The reserve is refilled afterwards. The Bank Loan printed on the board adds 2 successes in exchange for a Debt condition, unless the investigator already has Debt.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; EH_reference.pdf p.9, p.2 (Банковская ссуда)

### Component Action / Особое действие
An action printed after "Action" on the investigator sheet or on a possession. Each component at most once per round. A "Local Action" on a component of another investigator on the same space may also be performed.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; EH_reference.pdf p.7 (Особое действие), p.6 (Местное действие)

### Focus / Концентрация *(unofficial)*
Expansion action (Strange Remnants onward): gain 1 focus token, maximum 2. Spend 1 focus token to reroll 1 die during a test, any number of times.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.9

### Gather Resources / Сбор ресурсов *(unofficial)*
Expansion action (Masks of Nyarlathotep): gain 1 resource token, maximum 2. Resources are possessions; during Rest each spent resource recovers 1 extra health or sanity, during Acquire Assets each adds 1 success.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.10

## Encounters

### Encounter / Контакт
What an investigator resolves in the Encounter phase. Kinds: combat, location, general, research, other world, expedition, rumor, defeated investigator, special.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; EH_reference.pdf p.5

### Combat encounter / Боевой контакт
Mandatory encounter against each monster on the investigator's space, one at a time in the order of choice; non-epic monsters before epic ones. Steps: check monster effects, Will test (lose sanity by which horror exceeds successes), Strength test (lose health by which damage exceeds successes; the monster loses health equal to successes, simultaneously). A monster with health at or above its toughness is defeated. A missing test on the token is skipped.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3, p.8; EH_reference.pdf p.2

### Location encounter / Контакт в локации
Draw a card from the deck matching the space's artwork (its region) or from the general encounter deck, resolve the effect for the current space, discard.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; EH_reference.pdf p.5

### General encounter / Общий контакт
A location encounter drawn from the general encounter deck instead of the regional deck.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; EH_reference.pdf p.5

### Token encounter / Контакт жетона
Encounter offered by a token on the space: clue (research), gate (other world), active expedition (expedition), rumor, defeated investigator.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; EH_reference.pdf p.20

### Research encounter / Поисковый контакт
Token encounter at a clue, drawn from the research deck of the chosen Ancient One.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; EH_reference.pdf p.9

### Other world encounter / Контакт в Ином мире
Token encounter at a gate; a complex encounter with initial, pass and fail effects. "Close this gate" discards the gate token on the space.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3, p.4; EH_reference.pdf p.5

### Expedition encounter / Экспедиционный контакт
Token encounter at the active expedition token; after drawing, the token moves to the space on the back of the new top card.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3, p.4; EH_reference.pdf p.13

### Special encounter / Особый контакт
Encounter tied to the chosen Ancient One; resolution defined by the Ancient One sheet or mystery cards.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.4; EH_reference.pdf p.7

### Complex encounter / Составной контакт
Expedition, other world or special encounter: resolve the initial effect, then the pass or fail effect, then discard.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.4; EH_reference.pdf p.12

### Ambush / Засада
"A monster ambushes you": draw a random monster from the cup and fight it immediately; it is not spawned, returns to the cup afterwards, and defeating it grants no extra encounter.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; EH_reference.pdf p.4

## Tests and skills

### Skill / Навык
One of five: Lore (Знания), Influence (Общение), Observation (Внимание), Strength (Сила), Will (Воля). Value is the printed value plus improvement tokens (and minus impairment tokens where used).
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3, p.9; EH_reference.pdf p.6, p.12

### Test / Проверка
Roll dice equal to the skill value plus or minus the test modifier and other effect modifiers, minimum 1 die. Only the largest single skill bonus from a possession applies; extra dice from different effects add up. Each 5 or 6 is a success; at least one success passes. The number of successes is the test result.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3, p.8; EH_reference.pdf p.9, p.10

### Reroll / Переброс
Immediately after rolling, spend 1 clue to reroll 1 die, repeatable. Focus tokens reroll likewise where in play.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3, p.9; EH_reference.pdf p.8

### Improvement / Улучшение навыков
Token under the sheet, +1 then flipped to +2; a skill cannot be improved more than twice. Improvement and impairment tokens of one skill cancel.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3, p.9; EH_reference.pdf p.13

### Impairment / Ухудшение навыков *(unofficial)*
Expansion token, -1 then flipped to -2. A skill cannot be impaired below a modified value of 1 nor more than twice.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.9

## Investigator state

### Health and sanity / Здоровье и рассудок
Each sheet lists maximums; current values start at and never exceed them. Reaching 0 in either defeats the investigator. Health or sanity cannot be spent if doing so would defeat the investigator.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.4; EH_reference.pdf p.4

### Delayed / Задержанный сыщик
Token laid on its side; the investigator cannot perform actions. Becoming delayed during one's own Action phase ends the current action and forfeits the rest. In the next Action phase the token is stood up instead of acting.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.4; EH_reference.pdf p.4

### Defeated / Побеждённый сыщик
At 0 health or sanity: stop everything, advance doom by 1, move to the nearest city and lay the token down with a health token (lost health) or sanity token (lost sanity), discard conditions and health, sanity and improvement tokens, keep possessions on the set-aside sheet, pass the lead investigator token. At the end of the Mythos phase the player takes an unused investigator. After the Ancient One awakens the player is eliminated instead.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.4, p.8; EH_reference.pdf p.8

### Devoured / Сожранный сыщик
Advance doom by 1, discard all possessions, conditions and tokens, return sheet and token to the box, pass the lead investigator token. New investigator at the end of the Mythos phase, unless the Ancient One has awakened.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.4, p.8; EH_reference.pdf p.12

### Possession / Имущество
Assets, artifacts, spells, clues, travel tickets (and unique assets, resources where in play). Tradeable. Conditions are not possessions.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3, p.4; EH_reference.pdf p.5

### Condition / Состояние
Card with hidden back text, cannot be traded, no duplicates. Detained replaces the encounter; Poisoned blocks recovery from Rest.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.4; EH_reference.pdf p.12

### Spell / Заклинание
Possession with a hidden back; no duplicates; effects on the front resolve, the card says when it flips.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.4; EH_reference.pdf p.4

## Board

### Space / Локация
A place on the board: city (город), wilderness (глушь) or sea (море). Cities belong to a region. Numbered spaces are seas or wilderness as printed.
Source: EH_reference.pdf p.5; manual_eng.pdf p.3

### Region / Регион
Encounter grouping of spaces: America (Америка), Europe (Европа), Asia (Азия). Determines which location encounter deck a space uses.
Source: EH_reference.pdf p.5; manual_eng.pdf p.3

### Path / Путь
Connects two adjacent spaces; type train (поезд), ship (корабль) or uncharted (тропа). Expansions add local paths (местные пути, *unofficial*), along which an investigator may move once per round without an action unless delayed.
Source: EH_reference.pdf p.10; manual_eng.pdf p.3, p.9

### Adjacent / Соседние локации
Two spaces joined by one path.
Source: EH_reference.pdf p.12; manual_eng.pdf p.3

### Nearest / Ближайший
Distance between spaces is the number of paths on the shortest route; the nearest matching space is the one at the smallest distance.
Source: EH_reference.pdf p.2

### Random space / Случайная локация
The space shown on a clue token discarded from the pool; the lead investigator chooses if the pool is empty.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.4; EH_reference.pdf p.11

### Reserve / Резерв
Four faceup asset cards on the board available to Acquire Assets; refilled from the asset deck.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; EH_reference.pdf p.10

## Tokens and tracks

### Doom / Безысходность
Track from the Ancient One's starting value down to 0. Advancing moves the token toward 0; at 0 the Ancient One awakens.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3, p.4; EH_reference.pdf p.2

### Omen / Знамение
Token on the omen track; the current omen is its symbol. Advancing moves it clockwise, then doom advances by 1 per open gate matching the new omen.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; EH_reference.pdf p.4

### Reckoning / Расплата
Mythos effect: resolve every reckoning symbol in play in order monsters, Ancient One sheet, ongoing mythos cards, then each investigator's possessions and conditions clockwise from the lead investigator. Components gained during reckoning do not resolve.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3, p.4; EH_reference.pdf p.10

### Gate / Врата
Token from the gate stack placed faceup on its printed space; spawning a gate also spawns 1 monster there. Closing discards it; an empty stack and discard mean doom advances by 1 instead.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3, p.4; EH_reference.pdf p.3

### Clue / Улика
Token from the clue pool placed facedown on its printed space; a research encounter; spendable to reroll. Empty pool and discard mean no clue.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3, p.4; EH_reference.pdf p.13

### Gate stack / Стопка врат
The 9 core gate tokens (one per named city) shuffled facedown at setup; gates spawn from the top. Discarded gates form a faceup pile that is reshuffled into the stack when it runs out.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; EH_reference.pdf p.3, p.13

### Clue pool / Банк улик
One clue token per core space shuffled facedown at setup; clues spawn from the top and starting clues are taken from it. Discarded clues return facedown and are reshuffled when the pool is empty.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; EH_reference.pdf p.11, p.13

### Monster cup / Пул монстров
Opaque container holding every non-epic monster token not set aside; monsters spawn from it at random and return to it when defeated. Epic monsters never enter it.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; EH_reference.pdf p.13

### Reference card / Памятка
Card chosen by investigator count giving the number of gates to spawn, clues to spawn and monsters per surge: 1–2 investigators 1/1/1, 3–4 1/2/2, 5–6 2/3/2, 7–8 2/4/3.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; printed reference cards (mirrored in the mod's icon-reference-card-holder.js)

### Set aside / Отложенные монстры
Monsters the Ancient One sheet removes from the cup at setup for its own effects. They never return to the cup; when discarded they are set aside again.
Source: EH_reference.pdf p.13; Ancient One sheet backs

### Monster / Монстр
Token drawn at random from the monster cup, with horror and Will test, damage and Strength test, toughness, and back-side effects. Defeated monsters return to the cup; set-aside monsters never do.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3, p.4; EH_reference.pdf p.6

### Monster surge / Наплыв монстров
Mythos effect: at each gate matching the current omen, spawn the reference-card number of monsters; with no matching gate, spawn 1 gate instead.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; EH_reference.pdf p.6

### Epic monster / Эпический монстр
Red, clipped token spawned only by specific effects; defeated only by health reaching toughness; never in the cup, never discarded or moved except by its own effects; returned to the box when defeated.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.4, p.8; EH_reference.pdf p.13

### Rumor / Слух
Ongoing mythos card with a rumor token on a space; the token offers an encounter. Solving the rumor discards the card and everything it placed.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3, p.4; EH_reference.pdf p.11

### Eldritch token / Жетон Древних
Placed by mythos cards or components, which define how it is encountered; on the omen track's green space it does not replace the space.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3, p.4; EH_reference.pdf p.4

### Travel ticket / Билет
Train or ship ticket, possession, maximum 2; spent during Travel for extra moves on matching paths.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3; EH_reference.pdf p.8, p.10

## Ancient One and victory

### Ancient One / Древний
The chosen antagonist sheet: doom start value, mythos deck composition per stage, cultist stats, setup, reckoning and awakening effects, mysteries and final mystery.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3, p.4; EH_reference.pdf p.3

### Awakened / Пробудившийся Древний
When doom reaches 0 the sheet flips, Awakens effects resolve, the back replaces the front, and defeated or devoured investigators are eliminated.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.4; EH_reference.pdf p.3

### Mystery / Тайна
Card of the chosen Ancient One placed near the sheet; the active mystery collects tokens; solving 3 wins the game. After awakening the final mystery must be solved as well. The app draws the mystery and solves it when the players apply the card text; the enters-play effect and the tokens on the card are read from the image and handled by the players (deviation: card text is not modelled).
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3, p.4, p.9; EH_reference.pdf p.12

### Mythos card / Карта Мифа
Green, yellow or blue card with easy, normal or hard difficulty; Event cards resolve and discard, Ongoing cards stay in play. The deck is built in three stages from the Ancient One's counts and is never replenished.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.3, p.4, p.9; EH_reference.pdf p.6

### Winning and losing / Победа и поражение
Investigators win by solving 3 mysteries (plus the final mystery once awakened). They lose when the awakened sheet's condition is met, all investigators are eliminated, a card says so, or the mythos deck runs out.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.4; EH_reference.pdf p.8

## Expansion terms

### Prelude / Пролог *(unofficial)*
One prelude card drawn or chosen before setup and resolved immediately; may be skipped.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.9

### Unique asset / Уникальный актив *(unofficial)*
Double-sided possession with a hidden back, faceup deck beside the asset deck, tradeable, unlimited count; "asset" includes unique assets.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.9

### Adventure / Приключение *(unofficial)*
Card placed faceup in play when drawn; completing it resolves its completion effect and draws the next chapter.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.9

### Resource / Ресурс *(unofficial)*
Masks of Nyarlathotep token, see Gather Resources.
Source: manual_eng.pdf p.10
