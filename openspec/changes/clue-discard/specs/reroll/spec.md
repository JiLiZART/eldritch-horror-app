## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: A die is rerolled for a clue
While a roll waits, the rolling investigator SHALL spend 1 clue to reroll 1 die of their choice; the new face SHALL replace the old one, better or worse, and the result SHALL be recounted; this MAY be repeated for each clue held. The spent clue token SHALL join the clue discard. Without a clue the reroll SHALL be refused saying so. Rerolled faces SHALL come from the game seed so a replay repeats them. **Deviation**: focus tokens and possessions that reroll are the players' call.

#### Scenario: Rerolled
- **WHEN** Leo Anderson with 2 clues rerolls the first of his waiting Will dice
- **THEN** he holds 1 clue, the first die shows the new face and the successes are recounted

#### Scenario: Worse face kept
- **WHEN** a rerolled die lands lower than before
- **THEN** the new face stands

#### Scenario: No clue
- **WHEN** an investigator without clues rerolls a die
- **THEN** it is refused because a reroll costs 1 clue

#### Scenario: Nothing waiting
- **WHEN** a die is rerolled while no roll waits
- **THEN** it is refused because no roll is waiting

#### Scenario: Reroll clue discarded
- **WHEN** Leo Anderson rerolls a die for a clue
- **THEN** one clue token lies on the clue discard
